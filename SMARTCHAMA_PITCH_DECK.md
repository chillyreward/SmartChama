## SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM

### The Chama Challenge
- **  3 ,000+ active chamas** in Kenya alone manage over **$2 billion** in collective savings
- **Manual record-keeping** leads to disputes, fraud, and mistrust
- **No access to credit** - members can't borrow against their savings
- **Limited investment options** - groups miss out on high-yield opportunities
- **Offline-first reality** - 60% of members lack reliable internet access

### The Pain Points
1. **Trust Issues**: 23% of chamas dissolve due to financial disputes
2. **Inefficiency**: Treasurers spend 10+ hours/month on manual bookkeeping
3. **Missed Opportunities**: 89% of chamas keep funds in low-interest accounts
4. **Access Barriers**: Traditional banking excludes informal savings groups
